What we explored

We studied how oil prices, macroeconomic fundamentals and global financial conditions shape the Norwegian krone across different market environments.

The analysis covers 14 economies from 2000 to 2025. It examines whether the relationship between oil and the krone changes when currency markets become more volatile.
